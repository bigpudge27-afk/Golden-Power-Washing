About Golden Power Washing

Golden Power Washing is a mobile power washing company based in Berea, Ohio, providing residential and commercial exterior cleaning across the Greater Cleveland area.

Built around dependable service

Golden Power Washing was built to provide straightforward, reliable exterior cleaning for homeowners and businesses across Berea and surrounding Cleveland suburbs.

With 8+ years of experience, we understand what it takes to clean siding, concrete, patios, driveways, storefronts, and commercial surfaces efficiently while delivering a professional customer experience.

As a mobile service business, we bring the equipment directly to the job and make it easy to get a quote by phone or text. Our goal is simple: show up, communicate clearly, and leave your property looking better than when we arrived.
